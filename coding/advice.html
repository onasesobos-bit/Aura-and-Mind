Dealing with Exam Stress and Test Anxiety

Feeling nervous before a big test is completely normal, but it doesn't have to ruin your day.

Summary: Test anxiety happens when your brain goes into fight-or-flight mode. To fix this, try taking deep breaths, studying a little bit each night instead of cramming, and eating a good breakfast. Remember, one test does not define who you are!

How to Handle Drama with Friends

Friendships can get complicated in middle and high school. Here is how to keep your peace.

Summary: Drama spreads fast when people miscommunicate. If you are having an issue with a friend, try talking to them calmly in person instead of texting back and forth. Real friends will listen to your feelings.

Creating a Relaxing Bedtime Routine

Struggling to sleep because your mind won't turn off? A solid routine can help.

Summary: Looking at a glowing phone screen right before bed tricks your brain into thinking it is still daytime. Try turning off screens 45 minutes before sleep, reading a book, or listening to calm lo-fi music.
