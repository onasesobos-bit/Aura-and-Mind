Mental Health Matters

Between exams, social expectations, and figuring out who you are, being a teenager is intense. Your feelings are completely valid, and you never have to carry everything alone.

The 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Method

When anxiety or racing thoughts take over, use your senses to bring yourself back to the present moment. Look around and name:

• 5 things you can see
• 4 things you can touch
• 3 things you can hear
• 2 things you can smell
• 1 thing you can taste

Setting Healthy Boundaries

It is okay to say "no" to plans when you feel burnt out. Protecting your peace and mental energy isn't selfish—it is necessary for your long-term happiness and emotional health.

Journaling Out Your Thoughts

Sometimes your head feels crowded because you are holding onto too many worries. Do a "brain dump"—grab a notebook and write down everything bothering you without worrying about neat handwriting or grammar. Getting it out on paper helps clear your mind.
