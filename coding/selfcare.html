Your Ultimate Self-Care Guide

Taking care of yourself doesn't have to mean expensive spa days. Here are simple routines you can easily fit into a busy school week to protect your energy.

1. The Digital Detox Evening

Our phones keep our brains wired long after we log off. Try turning off social media notifications an hour before bed, and swap scrolling for reading a physical book or journaling your thoughts.

2. Move Your Body For Fun

Exercise shouldn't feel like a punishment. Put on your favorite upbeat playlist and dance in your room, take a quick walk around the neighborhood, or stretch out tight muscles after sitting at a desk all day.

3. Nourish and Hydrate

Dehydration is a sneaky cause of headaches, fatigue, and irritability. Keep a water bottle close by, and try eating colorful snacks like fruit or nuts to keep your energy levels steady through afternoon classes.

4. Create a Cozy Personal Space

Your physical environment affects your mood. Spend 10 minutes tidying up your desk or bed, light a nice candle (if safe!), or dim your overhead lights to create a warm, calm atmosphere to relax in.
